Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Growmate IT Company ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and your rights under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

1. Who we are

Growmate IT Company is the "data controller" for the information collected on this website. You can contact us at growmate35@gmail.com or on 07913 174310.

2. What information we collect

When you use our contact form, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number (optional)
  • The service you are interested in
  • The message you write to us

If you call us, email us or message us on WhatsApp, we also receive the details you choose to share with us.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and give you a quote
  • To arrange a meeting or call, if you ask for one
  • To provide the services you request
  • To keep simple records of our business

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not send marketing emails unless you ask us to.

4. Our legal reasons (lawful basis)

  • Your consent, which you give when you tick the box on our contact form.
  • Taking steps at your request before a contract, such as preparing a quote.
  • Legitimate interests, such as running and protecting our business.

5. Who we share your information with

We only share information with trusted services that help us run this website:

  • Web3Forms, which delivers your contact form message to our email inbox.
  • Google (Gmail), where we receive and store enquiry emails.
  • Content delivery services (such as jsDelivr, cdnjs and unpkg), which load website files and may see your IP address when you visit.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. When they do, they must protect your information to a standard that meets UK law.

6. How long we keep your information

If you send an enquiry and do not become a client, we keep your details for up to 12 months, then delete them. If you become a client, we keep records for as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal and accounting requirements.

7. Cookies

At the time of writing, this website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. If we add them in the future, we will update this policy and ask for your permission first.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong
  • Ask us to delete your information
  • Ask us to limit or stop using your information
  • Withdraw your consent at any time

To use any of these rights, email us at growmate35@gmail.com.

9. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, please contact us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
