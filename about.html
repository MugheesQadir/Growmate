Our Story

Professional Technology for Every Business

Growmate IT Company was started with one simple idea: every business, big or small, should be able to afford a professional online presence.

Many small businesses are told that a website costs hundreds or thousands of pounds, so they never get one. We wanted to change that. That is why our starter website is just £99, with domain, hosting and business email included.

We are a new company, and that means we work hard for every client. We listen, we explain things in simple words, and we do what we promise.
