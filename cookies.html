Cookie Policy

What cookies and similar technologies this website uses.

Last updated: October 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website can save on your device. Similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage, can also remember simple settings.

2. What we use

At the moment this website does not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies or analytics cookies. We only use your browser's local storage to remember your choice on our cookie notice, so we do not keep asking you.

3. Third-party services

To make the website look and work properly, we load some files from other providers:

  • Google Fonts for the text style used on the site.
  • Bootstrap (via jsDelivr) for page layout.
  • Font Awesome (via cdnjs) for icons.
  • Web3Forms, which receives your details only when you send the contact form.

When your browser loads files from these providers, they may receive technical information such as your IP address. Please see their own privacy policies for more details.

4. Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings at any time. If you do, the cookie notice may appear again on your next visit. Blocking some third-party files may change how the website looks.

5. Changes

If we start using analytics or other cookies in the future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

6. Contact us

Questions about cookies? Email growmate35@gmail.com. You can also read our Privacy Policy.
