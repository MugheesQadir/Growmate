Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Last updated: October 2026

Growmate IT Company ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and what rights you have under UK data protection law.

1. Who we are

Growmate IT Company provides business websites, mobile apps, business systems, booking systems, Meta ads and IT help. We decide how and why your personal information is used, so we are the "data controller". You can contact us at growmate35@gmail.com or on 07913 174310.

2. Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us, mainly through our contact form, by email, by phone or on WhatsApp:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number (if you give it)
  • The service you are interested in
  • The message and business details you send us

Like most websites, your browser also sends technical information (such as your IP address) to the services that deliver our website files, for example fonts and design libraries. See section 8.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry
  • To discuss your needs and give you a quote
  • To deliver the work if you become a client
  • To keep records and meet legal or accounting duties

We do not sell your information, and we do not send marketing emails unless you ask for them.

4. Our legal reasons for using your information

  • Consent: when you tick the box and send us the contact form.
  • Steps before a contract: when you ask us for a quote or a service.
  • Legitimate interests: to run and improve our business and keep it secure.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep records.

5. Who we share it with

We only share information with trusted services that help us run our business:

  • Web3Forms, which delivers contact form messages to our email.
  • Our email provider and, if you contact us that way, WhatsApp.
  • Professional advisers or authorities if the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry and any work that follows. If no work follows, we delete or anonymise your details after a reasonable time. Records about client work are kept for as long as the law requires.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of your personal information
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong
  • Ask us to delete your information
  • Ask us to limit or stop using your information
  • Withdraw your consent at any time
  • Ask for your information in a portable format

To use any of these rights, email growmate35@gmail.com. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

8. Cookies and third-party services

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts and design files from content delivery networks (Bootstrap, Font Awesome). When these load, the provider may receive your IP address. Read more in our Cookie Policy.

9. Keeping your information safe

We take sensible steps to protect your information, including using secure (HTTPS) connections and limiting who can see it. No online service is completely risk free, so please do not send sensitive information such as passwords or bank details through the contact form.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email growmate35@gmail.com or call 07913 174310.
